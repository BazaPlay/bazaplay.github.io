Circuit Flow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Circuit Flow ("the game", "we", "us") is published by BazaPlay and respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information the game itself stores

Your game progress — levels unlocked, stars, coins, hearts, streaks, purchased themes, and settings — is saved locally on your own device using standard on-device storage. We do not run our own servers and this data is never transmitted to us.

Advertising

The game displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process certain information, including your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and app interactions. This is used by Google to deliver and measure ads and, where applicable and permitted, to personalise them.

You can control ad personalisation and reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (Android). Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region where consent is required, the game presents a consent prompt before personalised ads are shown, and your choice is recorded on your device. You can change that choice at any time from the game's settings screen. Where consent is declined or withdrawn, ads may still be shown, but they will not be personalised.

Rewarded ads

Some rewards (such as refilling hearts) are offered in exchange for voluntarily watching an ad. Watching these ads is always optional.

Children's privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so it can be addressed.

Data security & your choices

Because progress is stored only on your device, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game removes it. No account or sign-in is required to play.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact us at you@example.com.
